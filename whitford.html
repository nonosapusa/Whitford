On a phone this opens the share sheet with the card already made — pick Mail and it arrives attached, nothing to drag. Addresses from the roster get copied to your clipboard at the same time, so paste them into To. On a computer there's no share sheet, so the file lands in your downloads and an addressed draft opens for you to attach it. Image instead of PDF shows up inside the message on an iPhone rather than as a file — easier for everyone to glance at, harder to print.

Three matches, one round. The front nine, the back nine and the full 18 are settled separately, so a blown front nine costs you $5 and nothing more — the back starts level again. Each nine is played hole by hole on net: win the hole, go one up. The Front, Back and 18 columns show where that match stands in holes, and whoever is up when the nine ends takes the stake. Teams plays better ball, each side counting its lowest net on the hole. Field gives every hole to the outright low net, and the most holes over the nine collects from everyone else. Every pair runs a 5-5-10 between each two players in the group. Ties push, and a match still in progress is marked *.

Course handicap = handicap index × slope ÷ 113 + (course rating − 72), rounded to the nearest stroke. Enter a plus handicap as +2.4. Red dots in a score box are the strokes that player receives on that hole, allotted by the stroke-index column shown for the selected tees. Net score subtracts only the strokes on holes you've actually posted, so a part-finished round still totals correctly.
